You are building a graffiti empire across the city by generating Creative Energy, producing Paint, and growing your Reputation. These resources unlock upgrades, boost production, and eventually trigger the game’s ending.

/////////////////////////

1. Getting Started

When the game begins, you start with:

🎨 Creative Energy: 0
🖌️ Paint: 10
🌆 Reputation: 0
🧑‍🎨 1 Mural worker
🧑‍🔧 1 Art Assistant

Resources automatically increase over time.

///////////////////////

2. Core Gameplay Loop

🔁 Passive Generation (Idle Income)
Every second:
Murals generate Creative Energy
Art Assistants generate Paint
Generators slowly boost production

You don’t need to click to progress — the game runs on its own.

🖱️ Manual Action
🎯 “Tag Wall” Button

Clicking adds:

+1 Creative Energy per click

Use this early to speed up progression.

/////////////////////////////

3. Resources Explained

🎨 Creative Energy
Primary progression resource
Used to unlock upgrades
Grows through murals + clicking

🖌️ Paint
Secondary resource
Used for certain upgrades
Generated by assistants

🌆 Reputation
End-game progression stat
Unlocks game completion at 100

⬆️ 4. Upgrades
Upgrades appear when you have enough resources.

Example upgrades:
🎨 Better Spray Cans → boosts Creative Energy production
🖌️ Vibrant Paint → boosts Paint production

Upgrade States:
🔒 Locked
🟡 Available
✅ Purchased

Once purchased:

Effects apply permanently
Production rates increase

//////////////////////////////////

⚙️ 5. Generators (Automation System)

Generators automatically produce resources:

🧑‍🎨 Graffiti Sprayer → boosts Creative Energy
🧑‍🔧 Paint Mixer → boosts Paint

They scale over time and stack with upgrades.

/////////////////////////////////

💾 6. Saving & Auto-Save
Game auto-saves every 30 seconds
Progress is saved on exit
Reloading restores your resources

///////////////////////////////

🧨 7. Reset System

🔄 Shift + R

Resets the game completely:

Resources reset
Upgrades reset
Production reset
Game restarts from the beginning

///////////////////////////////////

🏁 8. End Game

Once you reach:

🌆 100 Reputation

The game ends and displays:

“You turned the city into a living artwork.”

This marks your completion of the graffiti empire.

//////////////////////////////////

⚡ Dev Jump to End

High Creative Energy
